Maximum Principal Stress Theory

(Rankine Theory)

Failure occurs when the maximum principal stress reaches the yield strength. Best suited for brittle materials.

Formula: σ_eq = max(|σ₁|, |σ₂|) ≤ S_y / N

Maximum Shear Stress Theory

(Tresca Theory)

Failure occurs when the maximum shear stress equals the max shear stress at yielding in a uniaxial test. Conservative for ductile materials.

Formula: σ_eq = max(|σ₁ - σ₂|, |σ₁|, |σ₂|) ≤ S_y / N

Maximum Distortion Energy Theory

(Von Mises Theory)

Failure occurs when distortion energy per unit volume reaches the distortion energy at yield. Most accurate for ductile materials.

Formula: σ_eq = √(σ₁² - σ₁σ₂ + σ₂²) ≤ S_y / N
